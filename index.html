Core Capabilities

Initial Core Services

Focused engineering consulting services designed to provide rigorous technical scrutiny and decision support for critical mechanical hardware programs.

SERVICE // 01 — PRIMARY

Independent Structural CAE Review

A thorough third-party evaluation of customer-provided simulation models and reports to verify structural viability and surface overlooked engineering risks.

Scope of Review Includes:
  • CAD geometry & structural load paths
  • CAE reports & executive simulation findings
  • Modeling assumptions & idealizations
  • Applied loads & dynamic load cases
  • Boundary conditions & constraint validity
  • Mesh strategy, element quality & convergence
  • Material constitutive assumptions
  • Stress states & deformation results
  • Bolted joints, preloads & welded connections
  • Contact definitions & frictional slip
  • Correlation with available physical test results
Typical Deliverables:
  • Independent engineering assessment
  • Identification of modeling or structural risks
  • Risk prioritization matrix
  • Actionable design recommendations
  • Written engineering review memo
  • Technical review meeting with client team
SERVICE // 02

Mechanical Design Validation

Applying practical engineering judgment to help hardware teams resolve critical design questions before committing capital to production.

Key Questions We Help Answer:
  • Is the current design structurally reasonable? Evaluating stiffness, strength, and stress margins under operating and abuse conditions.
  • Which design changes are actually necessary? Separating critical structural vulnerabilities from cosmetic or low-risk flags.
  • Where are the primary structural risks? Pinpointing stress concentrations, joint weaknesses, and instability modes.
  • Does the proposed redesign address root causes? Ensuring counter-measures do not simply shift failure modes elsewhere.
  • What should be validated before tooling? Defining key physical test checkpoints to mitigate residual uncertainty.
Core Benefit:

Objective decision support to ensure redesign resources are targeted where they have the highest engineering impact.

SERVICE // 03

Targeted FEA Verification

When an independent review identifies a critical uncertainty or unresolved simulation discrepancy, we perform focused, high-resolution finite element simulations.

Verification Scenarios:
  • Local stress verification around critical cutouts or fillets
  • Bolted or welded joint structural assessment
  • Large structural deformation & buckling tendencies
  • Nonlinear geometric and material response
  • Comparative analysis of candidate redesign geometries
  • Test-to-simulation correlation and discrepancy resolution
Technical Methodology Note:
Our consulting approach is grounded in extensive professional engineering experience utilizing industry-standard simulation and preprocessing workflows (e.g., ANSYS, LS-DYNA, ANSA, META, and Creo). We apply sound numerical principles tailored to client project requirements.

Engineering Decision Support

Why an Independent Engineering Review Matters

Independent engineering judgment before committing to expensive redesign, tooling, or additional simulation cycles.

“An independent review helps engineering teams distinguish critical structural risks from lower-priority issues and focus resources on the design changes that matter most.”

In fast-paced product development cycles, in-house simulation teams and external vendors face tight delivery pressure. An independent, unaligned technical review provides an objective second opinion on assumptions that dictate product reliability.

01

Uncover Hidden Assumptions

Simulation results are only as valid as boundary conditions, contacts, and load paths. We audit the fundamental inputs that internal teams often take for granted.

02

De-risk Tooling & Prototyping

Modifying hardened steel injection molds or stamping dies costs weeks and tens of thousands of dollars. We catch structural flaws while changes remain virtual.

03

Objective Prioritization

Not every high-stress hotspot represents a genuine failure mechanism. We distinguish numerical singularities from true physical structural risks.

Engagement Workflow

How We Work

A transparent, five-step engineering review process structured to deliver clear, actionable findings with minimal overhead for your team.

PHASE // 01

Customer Information

Receipt of relevant CAD models, existing CAE reports, load specifications, test data, and documented problem statements under NDA.

PHASE // 02

Independent Engineering Review

In-depth audit of boundary conditions, mesh fidelity, contact formulations, material definitions, and load path mechanics.

PHASE // 03

Risk Assessment

Categorization of identified vulnerabilities by severity, probability of physical failure, and impact on functional performance.

PHASE // 04

Design Recommendations

Formulation of practical geometry modifications, joint alterations, or rib configurations aimed directly at root causes.

PHASE // 05

Review Memo & Discussion

Delivery of a concise, written engineering review memo followed by a technical debrief session with client engineering stakeholders.
